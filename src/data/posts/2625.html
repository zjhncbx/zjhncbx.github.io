 Weather Report



寒潮之後暂时个回温马上结束，小雪节气一到，又要开始降温咧。接落去个礼拜，夜裏顶低温度可能便得零度，还好日裏天晴，温度也有十来度，但是棉毛裤、棉毛衫要着起来咧，正式个冬天要来咧。









寒潮过后的回温来的异常突然，回温过后的气温下跌也随之而来。本周的气温较上周来说会有所下降，早晚间的最低温度更是要接近零下。虽然日间的温度基本为十几度，且都为多云、晴天。但是昼夜温差较大，晚上更是能达到零下的温度，大家一定要做好日常保温工作，不要让严寒影响个人健康状况。


The rise of temperature after a cold spell was unusually sudden, but the warming followed with a drop in temperature.The temperatures this week was cooler than last week, with lowest temperature  closing  to zero in the morning and evening. Although the temperature during the day was basically around 10˚ C , and the weather were mostly cloudy and sunny. However, the diurnal temperature variation was large, and the temperature at night could reach sub-zero. We must pay attention to keep warm, so as not to let the cold affect personal health.

空气质量

Air Quality


下周空气质量普遍为优，少许两天是良，空气质量较好，适合多出门走走，空气质量为良的天气记得带好口罩、涂好面霜，合理安排工作与休息的时间喔！


Next week the air quality will be generally good with a few days being fine.  The air quality will be quite well, so it is suitable to hang out more. Please remember to wear masks, apply good face cream these days and reasonably arrange time of work and rest.









生活指数

Living index









马上我们就要迎来十一月底的小雪节气了，天气也是突然就变得异常寒冷，大家也应该给自己的卫衣外多添一件外套，也可以适当的加上一件秋衣、秋裤抵御寒冷啦！


Soon we will usher light snow solar term in the end of November. The weather will suddenly become extremely cold. We should add a coat to our hoodie and appropriately add clothes and long johns to resist the cold!


观潮指数

Tide Index



下周的观潮综合条件较为一般，并且从二十一号起会有连续四天的大潮汛，大潮汛期间，大家一定要注意个人安全，千万不要在岸边逗留，切记：人生安全是头等大事!


Next week's tide watching conditions are relatively general, and there will be four consecutive days of tidal flood from 21st. During the tidal flood, you must pay attention to personal safety. Do not stay on the shore and remember: life safety is the top priority!
